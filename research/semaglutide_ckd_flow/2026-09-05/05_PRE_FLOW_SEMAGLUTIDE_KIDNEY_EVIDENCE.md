# 05. Pre-FLOW Semaglutide Kidney Evidence

## SUSTAIN-6 (`SUSTAIN6-2016`, Marso, NEJM 2016)

N=3,297 (1,648/1,649), T2D at high CV risk. Semaglutide 0.5/1.0 mg SC weekly, primary CV-safety trial: noninferiority was achieved; the MACE HR was **0.74 (0.58–0.95)**, with only post hoc/non-prespecified nominal evidence for superiority rather than a multiplicity-protected superiority claim.

**Renal composite (secondary/exploratory, not the primary outcome):** new persistent macroalbuminuria, persistent doubling of serum creatinine with eGFR≤45, continuous renal-replacement therapy, or renal death: HR **0.64 (0.46–0.88)**, P=0.005; 62/1,648 (3.8%) vs 100/1,649 (6.1%). Macroalbuminuria alone accounted for 2.5% vs 4.9% — the dominant component. **Exact event counts for the hard components (creatinine-doubling, RRT, renal death) individually are not recoverable from any source reached in this project** (NEJM full text 403-paywalled to every attempt); only the qualitative characterization "small and similar between groups" is available. This is the clearest illustration in the pre-FLOW literature of an **albuminuria-driven composite benefit**, distinct from a demonstrated **kidney-failure/hard-eGFR-decline benefit**.

**Retinopathy signal:** HR **1.76 (1.11–2.78)**, P=0.02; 50/1,648 (3.0%) vs 29/1,649 (1.8%). Time to first diabetic-retinopathy complication was a **prespecified, externally adjudicated secondary microvascular endpoint**, not merely a generic adverse-event report; it was not multiplicity protected, and the trial was not designed around serial retinal imaging or retinopathy-progression grading. Generic diabetic-retinopathy adverse events were collected separately. Locator for this endpoint definition: `SUSTAIN6-RETINOPATHY-2018`, Methods §2.1.4 and Results (DOI 10.1111/dom.13172; PMID 29178519; PMCID PMC5888154).

Locator for this section: `SUSTAIN6-2016`, primary publication Results (DOI [10.1056/NEJMoa1607141](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1607141); PMID [27633186](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27633186/)); the kidney-composite characterization is also reproduced in the primary open mediation article `SUSTAIN6-MEDIATION-2021`, Introduction/Methods §2.2 (PMCID PMC8453827). Exact hard-component counts remain unavailable and are not inferred.

## PIONEER-6 (`PIONEER6-2019`, Husain, NEJM 2019)

N=3,183 (1,591/1,592), oral semaglutide 14 mg daily, noninferiority CV-safety trial: MACE 61/1,591 (3.8%) vs 76/1,592 (4.8%), HR **0.79 (0.57–1.11)** — noninferiority met (P<0.001) but the CI crosses 1, so superiority was not demonstrated. Median follow-up only 15.9 months. **No dedicated renal composite. UACR was never collected at all** in this trial (confirmed across multiple independent secondary sources) — a key limitation for any pooled renal analysis drawing on PIONEER-6.

Locator: `PIONEER6-2019`, primary PubMed structured abstract/Results (DOI [10.1056/NEJMoa1901118](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1901118); PMID [31185157](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31185157/)); absence of UACR collection is independently stated in `SUSTAIN6-PIONEER6-EGFR-NDT-2025`, Methods.

## Pooled eGFR-slope evidence (`SUSTAIN6-PIONEER6-EGFR-NDT-2025`, Apperloo, NDT; online 2024, print 2025)

Post hoc pooled analysis, N=6,480 with eGFR data. Pooled eGFR-slope difference favoring semaglutide: **0.59 (0.29–0.89) mL/min/1.73m²/year**; SUSTAIN-6 alone **0.60 (0.24–0.96)**. Consistency across baseline HbA1c/BP/BMI strata (all P-interaction >0.5) and, in the SUSTAIN-6-only subset, across UACR strata (P-interaction 0.99) — but this is subgroup-consistency evidence (absence of detected effect modification), not proof of a glucose-/BP-/weight-independent mechanism (CLAUDE.md rule 7). The magnitude (~0.6 mL/min/1.73m²/yr) is roughly half of FLOW's own 1.16 mL/min/1.73m²/yr total-slope benefit, consistent with a lower-kidney-risk, non-enriched population.

Locator: [primary PMC article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11792646/), Results and Figs. 1–3 (DOI 10.1093/ndt/gfae150; PMID 38955363; PMCID PMC11792646).

`SUSTAIN6-PIONEER6-EGFR-NDT-2025` above asks a different question—whether **eGFR slope** varies by baseline HbA1c, BP, BMI, and UACR—and should not be cited as though it supplied the four threshold forest plots. Conversely, a percentage decline in eGFR is clinically meaningful but is not itself the same as dialysis, transplantation, persistent eGFR<15, or kidney death. The missing semaglutide-only forest-plot values remain an explicit acquisition gap rather than silently inferred numbers; both threshold analyses now have dedicated ledger rows.

## Formal mediation analysis (`SUSTAIN6-MEDIATION-2021`, Mann, Diabetes Obes Metab 2021)

The same mediation framework was applied **separately by trial** to LEADER (liraglutide, N=9,340, 605 kidney events) and SUSTAIN-6 (semaglutide, N=3,297, 162 kidney events); only the two semaglutide dose arms were pooled within SUSTAIN-6. Both analyses used a **different (CV-death-excluded) kidney composite than FLOW's own primary outcome**:

- HbA1c mediated: 25% (LEADER, 95% CI −7.1 to 67.3) / 26% (SUSTAIN-6, CI noncalculable)
- Systolic BP mediated: 9% (LEADER, 2.8–22.7) / 22% (SUSTAIN-6, CI noncalculable)
- Body weight mediated: 9% (LEADER, −7.9 to 35.5) / **0% (SUSTAIN-6, CI noncalculable; therefore uninformative rather than evidence of absent mediation)**

Most confidence intervals are wide or noncalculable. Each candidate mediator was analyzed separately, and the source explicitly warns that the resulting percentages cannot be added; **no joint three-factor mediation estimate was reported**. Each factor showed modest or variable, imprecisely estimated mediation (25–26% for HbA1c; 9–22% for BP), supporting only "suggestive, partial mediation" — not an unqualified "kidney benefit independent of glycemia/BP" claim. The semaglutide-specific body-weight point estimate was 0%, but its CI was noncalculable, the analysis used a different trial/composite than FLOW, and SELECT later estimated 81% (41–120%) weight mediation for a different surrogate — **week-104 eGFR change, using baseline and week 104 only, not eGFR slope**. Weight independence therefore remains **unknown within FLOW**; these non-FLOW analyses cannot be transported as a FLOW mediation estimate.

Locator: [primary PMC article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8453827/), Methods §§2.1–2.3, Results §3.2/Table 2, and Discussion (DOI 10.1111/dom.14443; PMID 34009708; PMCID PMC8453827).

## Pre-FLOW KDIGO-risk-category movement (`SUSTAIN6-KDIGO-2024`, Tuttle, Kidney Int Rep 2024)

Post hoc SUSTAIN-6 analysis: change in UACR was the main driver of favorable KDIGO risk-category movement; eGFR change drove progression mainly among the already-high-risk stratum — again consistent with an albuminuria-dominant pre-FLOW signal in a population with mostly preserved baseline kidney function, and therefore low power to demonstrate hard-endpoint reduction regardless of true underlying biology.

Locator: [primary PMC article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11284422/), Results/Figs. 5–6 and Discussion (DOI 10.1016/j.ekir.2024.04.028; PMID 39081763; PMCID PMC11284422).

## Bottom line

> Before FLOW, semaglutide's renal evidence was suggestive-to-hypothesis-generating: a secondary/exploratory nephropathy composite in SUSTAIN-6 dominated by reduced **new-onset persistent macroalbuminuria**, a short noninferiority trial (PIONEER-6) with no renal composite at all, post hoc directional signals across persistent ≥30%/≥40%/≥50%/≥57% eGFR-decline thresholds without a verified semaglutide-only forest plot in this project, a modest pooled eGFR-slope signal (~0.6 mL/min/1.73m²/yr, roughly half of FLOW's eventual magnitude), and a formal mediation analysis (on a different, non-diabetic-CKD-enriched population and composite) suggesting a non-trivial but imprecisely estimated glycemic/BP contribution. No prior trial enrolled a kidney-risk-enriched population for an adequately powered, adjudicated kidney-outcomes trial. FLOW filled that gap with a five-component primary composite **including cardiovascular death**; it was not powered to confirm kidney failure alone (see `02_FLOW_TRIAL_ANATOMY.md` and `03_FLOW_PRIMARY_OUTCOMES.md`).
