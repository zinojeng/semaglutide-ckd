## 5. Explicit numeric corrections carried into this inventory (do not re-introduce the retracted values)

1. **CV-death share of the FLOW primary composite.** Reject 37.2%, 41.2%, 39.4%, and any "35–40%" range as an *exact* first-event share. Table 2's five component rows overlap (summing the semaglutide-arm component counts gives 472, exceeding the 331 actual first-event total), so no exact share is derivable by arithmetic. Use only the source's own approximate statement — "cardiovascular death accounted for approximately 35% of the components of the primary endpoint" (`FLOW-SUPPLEMENT-2024`, Table S2 note, line 755) — and never describe this figure as a share of the treatment effect.
2. **FLOW-CKDSEVERITY-2025 (Mahaffey) Figure 2 values.** Use the source-image-verified matrix in `sources/LITERATURE_INGEST_REPORT.md`/`SOURCE_LEDGER.csv`. The local derivative was impermissibly mutated on 2026-09-05; reject its earlier misplaced eGFR≥60 and KDIGO values if encountered in transcripts or caches, and do not treat the modified file/front matter as an immutable correction record.
3. **Discontinuation figures.** AE-driven permanent discontinuation is 233 (13.2%) semaglutide vs 211 (11.9%) placebo; GI-specific permanent discontinuation (a subset) is 79 (4.5%) vs 20 (1.1%). These are distinct from the unresolved overall any-reason discontinuation figures — 26% (`FLOW-PRIMARY-2024`, pooled both arms) vs 28.8% (`FLOW-SGLT2-2024`, pooled both arms) — which remain a genuine, unresolved discrepancy between the two publications; do not harmonize them and do not relabel the AE-specific figures as "overall."
4. **Baseline MRA composition.** N=257 (not 259): spironolactone 218, eplerenone 38, esaxerenone 1, finerenone 0.
5. **Baseline SGLT2i hard-composite HRs.** In the 550 baseline-SGLT2i users, the primary (CV-death-inclusive) composite HR is 1.07 (0.69–1.67) and the kidney-specific (CV-death-excluded) composite HR is 1.18 (0.71–1.98). Neither directionally supports a retained/additive hard-kidney benefit; only eGFR-slope and UACR point estimates run in a supportive direction.
6. **FLOW enrolled-N discrepancy.** Design paper (`FLOW-DESIGN-2023`) reports N=3,534; the primary publication reports N=3,533 randomized. No checked source establishes the reason; both counts are preserved as source-specific.
7. **SELECT/FLOW/SOUL pooled analysis date.** PubMed records exact electronic publication on 2026-08-07 and ahead-of-print status at the 2026-09-05 cutoff. Raw Crossref metadata supplies only month-level `[2026, 8]`, with no day; `2026-08-01` was a tool-padding artifact, not a competing exact date. The earlier lane draft's "2026-01" was also incorrect.
8. **KDIGO 2026 status.** Public-review draft only; no final version identified at cutoff.
9. **EMA regulatory status.** Not an independent CKD indication; FLOW data live in SmPC §5.1, indication (§4.1) unchanged from standard T2D glycaemic wording. FDA and Taiwan TFDA both carry a standalone third indication bullet; the three jurisdictions must not be described as "substantively identical."
10. **FDA label version.** `FDA-OZEMPIC-APPROVAL-S025-2025` (2025-01-28) is the historical approval-chronology anchor; `FDA-OZEMPIC-USPI-S038-2026` (DailyMed revised 2026-05) is the current-wording anchor. Do not cite the cached, superseded 10/2025 label as current.
11. **Dialysis pooled-analysis denominator.** The source reports 34,064 participants, whereas SUSTAIN-6, SELECT, FLOW, and SOUL nominal randomized Ns sum to 34,084. No checked source explains the 20-person difference; retain “source-reported pooled analysis denominator 34,064” and never silently harmonize it.
