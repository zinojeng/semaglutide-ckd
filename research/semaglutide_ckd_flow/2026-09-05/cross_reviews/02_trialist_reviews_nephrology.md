## 2. Weakest claim requiring an exact correction: discontinuation figures are mislabeled as "overall," when they are AE-specific

**Location:** `03_nephrology.md` §6.2, final sentence: *"Overall permanent discontinuation of trial product: 233 (13.2%) vs 211 (11.9%)."*

**Problem:** these two numbers are not the trial's overall (any-reason) permanent-discontinuation rate. They are the count under the specific Table 3 row **"Adverse event leading to permanent discontinuation of semaglutide or placebo"** [`FLOW-PRIMARY-2024`, Table 3; also stated in prose: "Adverse events leading to permanent discontinuation of semaglutide or placebo were more common in the semaglutide group than in the placebo group (233 [13.2%] vs. 211 [11.9%]); this finding was driven mainly by discontinuation because of gastrointestinal disorders (79 [4.5%] vs. 20 [1.1%])."]. The trial's actual **overall (any-reason)** discontinuation figure is reported only as a single pooled-arms narrative statement — *"Semaglutide or placebo was permanently discontinued by 26% of participants during the trial"* [`FLOW-PRIMARY-2024`, prose, journal p.113, immediately after the vital-status/consent-withdrawal sentence] — which is **not** broken out by arm anywhere in the primary paper, and which is a materially different (larger, all-cause) quantity than the AE-specific 233/211 pair. A second, independently reported overall-discontinuation figure — *"permanent discontinuation of randomized treatment was reported in 28.8%"* [`FLOW-SGLT2-2024` (Mann et al. 2024), Nat Med, Results/Participant characteristics section] — is itself in tension with the primary paper's 26% (this is Discrepancy #2 already logged in `02_trialist_statistics.md` §11, independently of nephrology's memo).

**Exact correction wording (for nephrology lane, next revision):**

> Replace: *"Overall permanent discontinuation of trial product: 233 (13.2%) vs 211 (11.9%)."*
> With: *"Adverse-event-driven permanent discontinuation: 233 (13.2%) semaglutide vs 211 (11.9%) placebo [Table 3]. This is not the trial's overall (any-reason) discontinuation rate — the primary paper separately reports only a pooled-arm overall discontinuation figure of 26% [prose, not broken out by arm], while the companion SGLT2i-subgroup paper (Mann et al. 2024) reports 28.8% for the same trial-wide metric — a ~3-point discrepancy between the two papers that remains unresolved (see lane 02 discrepancy log) and should not be silently reconciled with either the 26% or the AE-specific 233/211 figures."*

**Why this matters:** as written, a reader of §6.2 could reasonably conclude that ~13% total attrition occurred, when the true any-reason attrition is roughly double that (26–29%). This doesn't change any efficacy conclusion (discontinuation was handled correctly for the ITT/in-trial-period estimand — see `02_trialist_statistics.md` §7), but it is a factual mislabeling that should not propagate into `10_SAFETY_ADVANCED_CKD_DIALYSIS.md` or `13_CLINICAL_DECISION_FRAMEWORK.md` discontinuation-counseling language.

## 3. Unresolved numerical/interpretive conflicts

### 3.1 CV death's share of primary-outcome events — three different percentages in play, none fully reconciled

Nephrology's §0/§1.1 reports CV death's share as **"123/331 (37.2%) of semaglutide events and 169/410 (41.2%) of placebo events — roughly 35–41% of all primary-outcome events, matching the trial's own statement of 'approximately 35%'"** [`FLOW-SUPP-2024`, Table S2 note]. My own lane (`02_trialist_statistics.md` §4/§11 Discrepancy #1) computed the **pooled** share differently: (123+169)/(331+410) = 292/741 = **39.4%**.

These are three distinct, legitimate-but-different quantities — semaglutide-arm-specific share (37.2%), placebo-arm-specific share (41.2%), and pooled share (39.4%) — none of which equals the source's own narrative "approximately 35%" [`FLOW-SUPP-2024`, line 755 in the local `.md`, Table S2 "Other considerations" row]. Nephrology's "roughly 35–41%" phrasing bridges all four numbers into one range without flagging that they answer slightly different questions (per-arm vs. pooled) and that even the pooled figure (39.4%) sits ~4.4 points above the trial's own stated "approximately 35%." **Neither lane has resolved the source of this gap** (possible explanations considered but not confirmed by either lane: a first-qualifying-event hierarchy vs. independent per-component counting, or a denominator that excludes some component overlap). Recommend the director's `12_EVIDENCE_GAPS_AND_CONTROVERSIES.md` state explicitly: *"CV death accounts for roughly 35–40% of FLOW primary-outcome events (exact figure depends on counting method: ~35% per the trial's own Supplementary Table S2, ~39% by pooled arithmetic on Table 2's component counts); the discrepancy is unresolved and immaterial to the qualitative conclusion that CV death is a large minority contributor, not the majority, of the composite."*

### 3.2 eGFR/UACR primary-outcome subgroup interaction P-values — confirmed absent from the publication itself, not just from the local extraction

Nephrology flagged this as `[GAP→lane 02]` (§4.2, §8 item 2): interaction P-values for the Fig. 2 eGFR/UACR (and other) subgroups are not in the local `FLOW_supplement_fulltext.md`, and nephrology asked me to confirm whether they exist anywhere in the Supplementary Appendix.

**Resolved, not merely reconfirmed as missing:** I re-opened `fulltext/FLOW.pdf` page 10 (Fig. 2, journal p.118) directly for this review and visually confirmed the published forest plot itself has **no interaction-P column or annotation at all** — only subgroup event counts and HR (95% CI) for every subgroup (sex, age, BMI, diabetes duration, HbA1c, region, race, ethnicity, eGFR, UACR, CV disease history, heart failure, insulin use, metformin use, SGLT2i use). A supplement-wide text search for the string "interaction" (`FLOW_supplement_fulltext.md`) returns exactly one hit, and it is a methods sentence about the eGFR-slope *linear model's* treatment×time interaction term — not a subgroup interaction-P value. **This is not a gap in our local fulltext conversion; the primary publication and its supplement genuinely do not report interaction P-values for the Fig. 2 subgroup panel.** (The only subgroup interaction P-values that do exist anywhere in this evidence set are for the *separately published* SGLT2i subgroup [`FLOW-SGLT2-2024`, P-interaction 0.109/0.100 etc.] and MRA/KDIGO subgroups [`FLOW-MRA-2025`, `FLOW-CVCKD-2025`], none of which correspond to the Fig. 2 eGFR/UACR panel of the primary paper.) Recommend closing nephrology's GAP #2/§8-item-2 as **resolved: confirmed absent from the source**, and noting in `12_EVIDENCE_GAPS_AND_CONTROVERSIES.md` that this specific gap cannot be filled from any locally available or plausibly obtainable primary source short of a data request to the trialists or the SAP/statistical appendix.
