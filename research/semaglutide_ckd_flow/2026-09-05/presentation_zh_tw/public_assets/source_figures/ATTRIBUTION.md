Endpoint boundary for the presenter: this SELECT main 5-component kidney composite comprises death from kidney causes, initiation of chronic kidney replacement therapy (dialysis or transplantation), persistent eGFR <15 ml min-1 1.73 m-2, persistent >=50% reduction in eGFR from baseline, or **new-onset persistent macroalbuminuria**. It **does not include cardiovascular death**. Therefore, do not describe it as structurally identical to FLOW's CV-death-inclusive primary composite or use it as direct confirmation of FLOW.
