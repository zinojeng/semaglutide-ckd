# Session brief — Senior nephrologist

Read `CLAUDE.md`, the master prompt, all local FLOW material, and available primary/official evidence. Write `lanes/03_nephrology.md` for a nephrology-specialist audience.

Focus on questions an endocrinology summary often misses:

- whether FLOW establishes protection against clinically important kidney progression versus an albuminuria/CV-death signal;
- eGFR slope interpretation, kidney failure components, competing death, creatinine bias from weight/muscle change, cystatin-C triangulation;
- albuminuria as marker, mediator, surrogate, or mechanism;
- represented versus unrepresented CKD phenotypes and absolute benefit across kidney risk;
- applicability to UACR <100, eGFR <25, non-diabetic CKD, glomerular disease, kidney failure, dialysis, transplant, frailty and sarcopenia;
- CKD-specific adverse-event management, sick-day/dehydration risk, insulin/SU hypoglycemia, nutrition and retinopathy;
- a kidney-first critique of sequencing/layering with RASi, SGLT2i, and finerenone.

Label facts, inferences, and unanswered questions separately. Supply claim-ready wording and exact source locators.
