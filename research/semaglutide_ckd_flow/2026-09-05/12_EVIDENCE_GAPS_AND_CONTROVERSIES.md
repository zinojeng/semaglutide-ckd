## B. Rejected/retracted arithmetic (do not reintroduce)

- CV death as 37.2%, 41.2%, or 39.4% of FLOW's primary-composite first events, or any exact "35–40%" range — invalid because Table 2's component rows overlap and are not a mutually exclusive first-event partition (`03`). Use only the source's own "approximately 35% of the components of the primary endpoint" language, and never as a treatment-effect share.
- Any stratum-specific (KDIGO/eGFR/UACR/SGLT2i/MRA-subgroup) ARR/NNT that is not explicitly published by the source — none should be back-calculated from crude event proportions (CLAUDE.md rule 8). The kidney-specific (CV-death-excluded) composite has **no published NNT anywhere in this evidence base**, at the trial level or by any stratum, and none is calculated here.
- Directional claims that post-randomization SGLT2i-initiation asymmetry biased FLOW's SGLT2i-subgroup HR "toward the null" (i.e., made it artificially conservative) — the direction and magnitude of any such bias cannot be established from initiation proportions alone, because initiation timing, indication, and prognosis are all time-dependent and non-randomized.

## C. Exploratory subgroup signals flagged, not suppressed, and not over-read

- All-cause death by UACR in `FLOW-CKDSEVERITY-2025`: <300 mg/g HR 1.17 (0.83–1.65, numerically worse) vs ≥300 mg/g HR 0.70 (0.57–0.85); P-interaction=.01 — one of 15 uncorrected interaction tests; the trial's own authors judge it likely due to chance, and this project adopts that judgment while recording the signal rather than omitting it.
- Nonfatal MI by eGFR in the same paper: P-interaction=.04, opposite-direction pattern, same "likely chance" adjudication.
- Mortality by UACR in `FLOW-CKDSEVERITY-2026-CJASN`: lowest at UACR≥2,000 mg/g, HR 0.47 (0.31–0.70), P-interaction=.02 — exploratory, hypothesis-generating; higher UACR identifies higher baseline risk but must not be described as conferring a larger *absolute* semaglutide benefit without published stratum-specific absolute-risk data (none exists).

## E. Statistical/methodological controversies carried forward

1. **Early-stopping bias.** FLOW crossed its prespecified efficacy boundary at the ~570-event interim analysis; the DMC then recommended stopping, and 741 of 854 planned primary-outcome events had accrued by final database lock. The primary-outcome estimate is adjusted for the group-sequential design; the three confirmatory secondary estimates (eGFR slope, MACE, all-cause death) are not. No bias-corrected (e.g., median-unbiased) re-estimate exists in any source reviewed. All point estimates should be read with the standard caveat that early-stopped trials can somewhat overstate the true long-run effect size — this is a general, trial-level caveat and does **not** support a claim that kidney-failure components matured differently from CV-death/MACE components in calendar time; no source in this project supplies component-level accrual-curve data to test that hypothesis, and it should be treated as an open question, not a demonstrated explanation for the individually nonsignificant hard-kidney-failure components.
2. **Multiplicity.** Every subgroup analysis, the kidney-specific composite, individual primary-outcome components, and all safety comparisons are explicitly not multiplicity-adjusted per the primary paper's own methods statement.
3. **Cross-trial comparator table is contextual only.** The reconstruction in `14` was checked against the primary publication or an openly available prespecified primary/secondary analysis for each trial; cells not re-verified are explicitly `NR`. Differing endpoint definitions (some composites include CV death, FIDELIO-DKD/FIDELITY kidney composites do not), eGFR/UACR floors, diabetes requirements, acute-dip handling, background-therapy eras, follow-up, and event rates mean these trials cannot be ranked against FLOW by hazard ratio alone (master prompt §XXIV, CLAUDE.md rule 9).
4. **Mediation vs. subgroup consistency are different questions.** A nonsignificant subgroup interaction is evidence of *no detected effect modification*; it is not a mediation analysis and does not establish that a given factor (glucose, weight, BP, UACR) is causally independent of the treatment effect (CLAUDE.md rule 7). No FLOW-specific UACR-mediation or BP-mediation analysis has been located anywhere in this evidence base — genuine gaps.
5. **REMODEL registry** (`REMODEL-REGISTRY-2026`; mechanistic trial of semaglutide in kidney disease, NCT04865770): results first posted to the registry 2026-01-14; design/methods papers exist but **no peer-reviewed primary-results paper had been identified as of the 2026-09-05 cutoff**. Registry/conference/sponsor-level findings must not be upgraded to peer-reviewed evidence in any deliverable.
6. **`SMART-NONDIABETIC-2025`** is a short-term (24-week), surrogate-endpoint (UACR) RCT in non-diabetic CKD — it demonstrates a 52.1% placebo-adjusted surrogate biomarker response, not a hard-outcome effect, and must not be read as establishing kidney-failure prevention in non-diabetic CKD.
7. **Badve class meta-analysis safety is mixed rather than uniformly reassuring.** Serious adverse events did not differ (RR 0.95, 95% CI 0.90–1.01; I² 88.5%), but adverse-event discontinuation was more frequent with GLP-1RAs (RR 1.51, 1.18–1.94; I² 96.3%). These high-heterogeneity class estimates do not replace FLOW's semaglutide-specific safety denominators (`GLP1-CLASSMETA-BADVE-2025`, structured abstract, Findings; DOI 10.1016/S2213-8587(24)00271-7, PMID 39608381).
